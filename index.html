Payments

Drop in the payment slip. The details fill themselves in.

Staff add the customer's bank or card slip. Orbique reads the amount, date and reference, and files the money where it really went.

  • Card goes to Stripe, online transfer to the bank, cash to the staff member who took it
  • Card payments show the amount after Stripe's 4% + RM 1 charge
  • One slip that paid deposit and rent splits into both, each with its own receipt

Summons

Know about a summons before the customer has left the country.

Orbique checks every plate with JPJ and PDRM twice a day and matches each summons to the booking that had the car on that date.

  • The renter who had the car is named on the summons automatically
  • Photos of the notice stay private, seen only by signed-in staff
  • Paid, disputed and passed-on summons tracked to the end

Tracking

See where every car is, and which ones are standing still.

GPS trackers report into the same screen as the bookings, so a car that is out has a renter's name next to its dot.

  • Live position, speed and last report for each car
  • A car that should be home but is not stands out at once
  • Trips kept per booking for disputes over mileage

Deposits

Refund the deposit with every deduction written down.

Each deposit follows the car: held when it goes out, checked when it comes back, refunded with a statement the customer can read.

  • Fuel, cleaning, damage and summons deducted line by line
  • A deposit statement is ready to send as soon as it is refunded
  • Cash still held by staff is listed until it is banked
